Why It Keeps Running

From the inside it feels like thinking. Most of that time you are trying to protect yourself.

If you understood this two years ago and it has happened a few times a week since, that is a few hundred times.

Every time, you come out with one more reason to wait or one more version of what to say.

You’ll know the difference by what you walk away with. If you were thinking, you have something to act on or test. If you were protecting yourself, you have one more thing to do first.

That’s why you haven’t been able to work this out on your own. You’re protecting yourself, so you already know which explanations to throw out. Advice from a coach or a book gets thrown out the same way.

Even “stop overthinking” becomes fresh ammunition.

Someone else can point out the reasons you skip, because they aren’t the one being protected. Once it’s in writing, those reasons stay where you can see them.

Questions

Straight answers

Can’t I get this from ChatGPT?

You can, and it may be enough if you already see everything that keeps the pattern going. It works off what you tell it, and you leave some of that out. I note what you left out, and I point to it again the next time the pattern starts.

What qualifies you?

Nothing formal. I have been teaching myself this subject for two years, and the essays here show how I work. I work from what you write. Once you are protecting yourself it is hard to spot your own excuse, and with it written down another person can see the point where the trigger turns into one. The free review lets you judge that on your own case before you pay anything.

Is this therapy?

No. This is not therapy and should not be a substitute for it. If you are in crisis or have been diagnosed with a condition, please see someone who is licensed. If your application reads that way to me, I will let you know.
