The Mechanism

Why it keeps running after you understand it

From the inside, the spiral feels like thinking. Usually it’s closer to defense. It keeps you from reaching a decision by handing you one more reason to wait.

The difference is direction. Thinking moves you toward something you can test or decide. Defense keeps giving you another box to tick before you get there.

That’s why figuring it out on your own hasn’t worked. The defense is doing the figuring, so it decides which explanations count. Coaching gets absorbed the same way.

Defenses don’t disarm themselves. They’ll use “stop overthinking” as fresh ammunition too.

If you understood this two years ago and it still runs a few times a week, it has run a few hundred times since.

Someone outside can see the reasons you skip, because they’re not the one you’re defending. Writing it down keeps those reasons on the page long enough to catch.

No. It’s not therapy and it doesn’t take the place of it. If you’re in crisis or dealing with a diagnosed condition, please see a licensed professional. I’ll point you in the right direction during your free review.

Why writing?

Because when we talk, we gloss over things. We speed up, or give the answer we think we’re supposed to give. Writing slows you down.
