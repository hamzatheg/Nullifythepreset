What happens to what you send

Your application is sent to me through a third-party form service (Web3Forms). Only I read it. I use it to write your breakdown and for no other purpose.

I use AI tools (currently Claude) to help me analyse what you send and draft your breakdown. Before anything goes in, I remove your name, contact details and anything else that identifies you. I read and edit every breakdown myself before it reaches you, and nothing you send is used to train AI models.

I don’t sell it or share it, and I don’t use any of it publicly without your permission.

You can ask me to delete your application and my breakdown, and I will. Write to official@nullifythepreset.com.

The form also logs which link or ad brought you here, so I can see where applications come from. A draft of your answers is kept on your own device while you write and is removed once you send.

If we work together after the review, messages go through Signal, which is end-to-end encrypted.
