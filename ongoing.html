If We Keep Working

You message me when it starts and I take it apart with you

This is for overthinking and second-guessing that you already understand and still find yourself doing. You have likely figured it out more than once and still lost days to the same loop, because it is hard to catch yourself while you are inside it. So you message me when it starts and I take it apart with you, every time. The aim is that it takes up less of your day and you get out of it faster.

Everything happens in a private Signal channel, end-to-end encrypted. If you don’t already use Signal, setup takes two minutes.

It is all in writing. When we speak, we tend to go through things too fast, or we say what we think we are supposed to say. You slow down when you write.

Describe what is going on as truthfully as you can, even where it doesn’t make sense. The things that don’t make sense are usually what we want to look at.

The Method

Breaking the machinery.

You can calm it down, and it helps for a while. The harder part is understanding what lets it come back.

01 — Find

The load-bearing parts

I look for what allows the same pattern to form again in situations that have nothing else in common.

02 — Test

Which ones it runs on

You change one thing in a real situation and report back to me what happened. Typically only a couple of things are keeping the pattern going. The rest is noise.

03 — Rebuild

Fewer permissions

Permissions are the things you allow yourself in the moment. One more check, or one more rewrite. We reduce these and set clear stopping points.
